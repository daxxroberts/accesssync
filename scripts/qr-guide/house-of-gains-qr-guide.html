Your QR Door Code

How to get in

This file is not your code. Your door code is a QR code that House of Gains sends you by email. If you bought a pass, it arrives within about a minute of checkout in a message titled "Your House of Gains day pass is ready." If it isn't in your inbox, check spam.

Three steps at the door

1
Open the code on your phone.It's in the email and also attached as an image, so you can save it to your photos. Turn your screen brightness all the way up.
2
Hold your phone 4 to 8 inches below the reader.Aim for the spot under the light on the Kisi Terminal Pro at the entrance. Hold it still, screen facing up at the reader.
3
Wait for the green flash.When the reader flashes green, the door is unlocked. Pull it open and you're in.

Good to know

Every code has an end time.The email that carried your code says exactly when it stops working. A pass starts the moment you buy it, not at your first scan and not at opening time. A 1-Day Pass runs 24 hours from checkout.
Want more days? Raise the quantity.1-Day Pass x1 = 24 hours. 1-Day Pass x2 = 48 hours. 1-Day Pass x3 = 72 hours. You get one code for all of it, starting at checkout and running straight through. It can't be paused or saved for later.
One code, one person.Anyone holding your code can open the door until it expires, so keep it to yourself. Bringing a friend? They buy their own pass and get their own code.

If the reader doesn't catch it

  • Brightness all the way up, and hold the phone still, 4 to 8 inches below the light.
  • Move out of direct sun or glare. Wipe the reader if it looks smudged.
  • Code expired? Your email says when it ended. A new pass sends a fresh code.
  • No email after a few minutes? Check spam, then reply to your order confirmation or contact the gym.
